# Write then verify application workflow

This is the application protocol for newly queued jobs. The app freezes the posting and an approved career snapshot before drafting. The original evidence quotes remain the audit record. A verified clean-text sidecar may correct spelling, case, punctuation, hyphenation, and spacing without changing words, order, numbers, dates, figures, or the case-insensitive identity of a proper name. The writer and converter see the clean text; truth checks read both layers.

## Handoffs

1. Requirements extraction reads the frozen listing in bounded pages. A separate audit checks the extracted rows against the same listing. An adjudicator resolves disputed units for that epoch. At most two re-epochs are permitted before a typed human stop names the remaining units.
2. Each of N independent candidates, default N = 5, gets a fresh write chat. It sees the frozen posting, requirements and listing quotes, and every approved career unit grouped by authority entity with IDs beside the text. Its first working step lists 6–10 strongest true units with IDs and one-line reasons. That shortlist stays in ignored run evidence and never enters the converter input or final documents. The writer then returns a plain-text résumé and letter using this brief: write the best honest one-page résumé and cover letter; lead with the strongest true evidence; use specific, natural English; let the letter add what the evidence states beyond the résumé; never claim more than a quote states. The prompt gives a per-job range derived from the measured layout capacity.
3. A fresh convert chat for that candidate maps the plain text to the production structured résumé and letter, attaching allowed evidence IDs. It changes wording only where the shape requires it. Content corrections continue in this convert chat, at most three rounds. The host records exact rejected responses and check IDs in ignored evidence.
4. Every applicable production truth and hygiene check runs on each structured candidate and its cited quotes. The selection receipt checks do not apply because the writer receives the full approved catalog. Review-stage-only artifacts are not produced, so their checks are N/A. Measured one-page fit runs after content gates and has a separate cap of four rounds, using production feedback. Non-converged candidates are dropped with their failing IDs.
5. If several candidates pass, one blind judge chat sees the posting, hand, accepted texts and page PNGs, and ranks all accepted candidates using the play-the-hand panel rubric. One judge handoff covers up to six; larger sets use pairwise elimination. A sole accepted candidate needs no judge. No accepted candidate leads to a typed human stop.
6. A fresh fact-check chat reviews the top-ranked candidate against every cited quote. A truth finding blocks it. The convert chat gets up to three normal content-correction rounds, every gate reruns, flagged spans must change or be removed, and a fresh recheck chat must find no remaining or new truth finding. Failure moves to the next ranked candidate. Each fact-check and recheck has a new chat key.
7. The app saves the accepted package, renders the PDFs and page images, and retains its generation and rejection evidence outside tracked source.

For N = 5, the candidate baseline is ten writer/converter handoffs, plus the variable requirements handoffs, one judge when at least two pass, and at least one fact-check. Each content or fit correction adds a handoff in its candidate's convert chat; a recheck adds a fresh chat. A candidate's write, convert, judge, fact-check, and recheck roles never share a chat key.

## Gates and guidance

Hard gates protect schema and shape, cited-quote grounding, role strength and ownership, dates, numbers, names, tools, skills, projects, education, attribution, trusted fields, target-company binding, argument-to-evidence binding, and measured one-page fit. The cited quote must support the actual claim. An intent to reduce expense supports purpose wording; it does not establish an accomplished result. Host-owned fields and evidence IDs cannot be invented by a chat.

The play-the-hand rubric, stylistic cadence, opening and closing quality, evidence selection, and ordinary prose edits are advisory writing and ranking criteria. They do not turn a truthful claim into a deterministic rejection. A supplied argument span or job-need quote must still be an exact substring of its stated source. Pick the two or three strongest proofs for the letter. The first proof-bearing paragraph establishes the link in the writer's own natural wording; at most one paragraph needs an explicit transfer frame.

Among the functions the app requires of a cover-letter paragraph are these: a claim phrase and a sentence relating its proof to the job need in the first proof-bearing paragraph of the letter, a proof sentence in every proof-bearing paragraph, an opening that leads with the target work, with the candidate’s role stated wherever a prior employer or named project is first named, the closing’s asset, action and target parts, and paragraphs long enough to stay within the letter’s proportion limits. A correction may remove a flagged detail unless a function the app requires of that paragraph would be lost.

## Limits

The bridge request and tool-result limits are 2 MiB each. V2 reserves 128 KiB for framing in either direction. The ChatGPT reply cap is 16,384 tokens. The prompt budget is 250,000 characters; a synthetic live checksum ramp delivered and answered 300,000 characters intact, leaving 50,000 characters of headroom. A prompt over budget fails with a typed limit error and is never silently truncated. These values are enforced in `electron/ipc/applicationV2Limits.js`.

An older-protocol job is read-only and reports: “This application was made by an older version, so regenerate it.” New jobs use v2 only.
